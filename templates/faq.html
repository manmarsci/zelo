Frequently Asked Questions

What are branded leftover suits and patches?

Branded leftover suits are authentic, original pieces from Pakistan's top brands (like Gul Ahmed, Khaadi, Sapphire, etc.) that remain after retail seasons. These are 100% genuine products sold at discounted prices. Patches are embroidered or printed fabric pieces used for embellishment, often sold separately for custom designs.

Do you deliver across Pakistan?

Yes! We deliver to all major cities across Pakistan including Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, and all other cities through our trusted courier partners (PostEx, TCS, Leopards, CallCourier).

How can I pay for my order?

We offer multiple payment options:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Pay when you receive your order
  • Bank Transfer: Direct transfer to our bank account
  • JazzCash/EasyPaisa: Mobile wallet payments

How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive an SMS/WhatsApp with the tracking number. You can track your order by:

  • Using the tracking link we send you
  • Visiting our "Track Order" page
  • Contacting us on WhatsApp with your order number

Are the products original and authentic?

Yes! All our products are 100% authentic branded leftovers. We source directly from authorized distributors and manufacturers. Every piece is inspected for quality before dispatch.

What is your delivery time?

Delivery times vary by location:

  • Major Cities: 2-4 business days
  • Other Cities: 4-7 business days
  • Remote Areas: 7-10 business days

What are your delivery charges?

Delivery charges are PKR 150 for orders below PKR 3,000. Orders above PKR 3,000 qualify for FREE delivery across Pakistan.

Can I exchange or return my order?

Yes! We accept exchanges within 7 days of delivery if the product is unused, unwashed, and in original condition with tags intact. Please see our Returns & Exchanges page for detailed policy.
