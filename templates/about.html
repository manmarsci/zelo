What Are "Branded Leftovers"?

You might be wondering: What exactly are branded leftovers, and are they authentic?

Branded leftovers are genuine, original products that remain after a brand's retail season ends, or surplus stock produced during manufacturing. These are 100% authentic items featuring the exact same premium fabric, intricate embroidery, and high-quality prints you would find in a brand's flagship store.

Because they are surplus stock, we are able to offer them to you at steep discounts. Sometimes you get a complete 3-piece suit, and sometimes you might find unique single pieces, patches, or lace bundles. Every item is a unique treasure!
